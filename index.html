Line them up!

The goal of each puzzle is to create lineups that sort the enemies into two kinds of groups: squares, or lines.

Note: Enemies can't be a part of multiple groups at once.

Move enemies around by rotating rings, or by sliding rows.

Switch between moving rings or rows by pressing X. Once you've got a ring or row you'd like to change, press Space to select it. After that, use your Arrow Keys to move them around. Press Space again to confirm your move, and you're done! You can also press Z to undo.

Try to sort all of the enemies in as few moves as possible!
